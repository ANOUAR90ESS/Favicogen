## 1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy
Logo & Favicon Studio ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is designed to provide high-quality vector logo creation, favicon generation, YouTube branding kits, image trimming, and icon customization. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of all our users.

This Privacy Policy explains how our Application operates, how your data is handled, and our compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), and GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation).

---

## 2. Data Processing Architecture (Client-Side Processing)
- **Local On-Device Execution:** The core functionality of Logo & Favicon Studio runs locally on your client device using HTML5 Canvas, WebGL, and client-side JavaScript.
- **No Asset Uploading:** Your imported images, custom logos, trimmed photos, color choices, and exported design files are **NOT** uploaded to or stored on our external servers.
- **Local Storage:** Project states, custom templates, and user preferences are stored exclusively in your device's browser `localStorage` or `IndexedDB`. You can clear this data at any time through your browser or device settings.

---

## 3. Optional AI Features (Google Gemini API)
If you choose to use the optional AI Logo & YouTube Banner Generator:
- Your entered text prompt and any voluntarily attached reference image are sent securely to Google's official Gemini API endpoints (`@google/genai`).
- These requests are used strictly to generate the design suggestion you requested and are not retained or utilized for personal profiling.

---

## 4. Permissions & Device Access
The Application only requests permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:
- **Storage / File System (Read & Write):** Required solely to let you select an image from your device and download exported design files (PNG, SVG, ICO, JPG, WebP, ZIP packages).
- **Network / Internet:** Used only for optional cloud services, Google Gemini AI design suggestions, and web fonts.

We do **NOT** request access to:
- Precise or coarse location data
- Contacts or phone book
- Microphone or camera
- Device ID or advertising ID
- SMS or call logs
